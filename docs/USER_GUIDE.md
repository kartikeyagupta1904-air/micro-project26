# Granthalaya user guide

## Signing in
Librarians use their librarian ID. Students use their college roll number (for example BTCS24O1001: BT, branch CS, joining year 24, serial 001). After five wrong attempts, sign-in pauses for 30 seconds. Change your password with the lock icon in the sidebar (8 or more characters, with letters and numbers).

## Library rules
- Loans last 14 days.
- Each student can hold 3 books at a time.
- A late return costs Rs. 2 for every day after the due date.

## Librarian tasks
- **Add or edit a book:** Books, then Add book, or Edit on a cover or row. Total copies cannot go below the number currently issued.
- **Register a student:** Students, then Register student. Choose branch and year and leave the roll number blank to get the next free one, or type it. Set a starting password and share it privately.
- **Find students:** Students has branch and year filters next to the search box. Reports, then By branch, shows students, loans and overdue books per branch.
- **Reset a password:** Students, then Reset password on the student's row.
- **Issue a book:** Issue & return. Enter the roll number and book ID (suggestions appear as you type). An issue slip shows the due date.
- **Return a book:** Same page. The receipt shows days late and the fine.
- **Reports:** all records, one student's history, overdue books and statistics. Use Export records (CSV) to open the data in Excel or Sheets.

## Student tasks
Browse the catalogue under Browse (pick your branch to see its textbooks, plus genre chips) and see your own loans, due dates and fines under My books. The dashboard's Due soon panel shows your next returns.

## Appearance
Click the round palette button at the bottom-right to change the colour scheme (Midnight, Sunset, Emerald, Violet), mode (Auto, Light, Dark) and moving wallpaper (Aurora, Network, Books, Waves, Plain), or to switch animations off. Static wallpapers for your desktop or phone are in `frontend/assets`.

## Keeping your data safe
Data lives in the browser on the computer you use. Export records often, and avoid clearing site data for this page.
